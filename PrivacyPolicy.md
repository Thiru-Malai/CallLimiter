# 📜 Privacy Policy – Call Limiter App

_Last updated: May 1, 2025_

Thank you for using **Call Limiter**. Your privacy is important to us. This document outlines the information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 📱 What Does the App Do?

**Call Limiter** helps users manage and limit call durations to specific contacts. It includes features like:

- Setting time limits for phone numbers  
- Automatically disconnecting calls once time limit is reached  
- Tracking daily talk time  
- Providing a buffer and emergency call duration  
- Auto-resetting timers every day  

---

## 🔐 What Information Do We Collect?

We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal data or audio content. The app runs entirely on your device.

However, to function properly, the app requires access to:

- Your phone's call status to detect when a call starts or ends  
- Outgoing call details to monitor calls made to specific numbers  
- The ability to disconnect calls when a time limit is reached  
- Call handling capabilities for advanced features on supported Android versions  
- Notifications to display ongoing call timers and alerts  
- Background services to ensure timers work reliably even when the app is not open  

These access permissions are strictly used to support the core functionality of the app and no data is sent externally or stored remotely.
